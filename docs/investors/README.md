## Positioning note (construction raises)

Most angels/VCs underwrite **growth equity or structured capital** for regional contractors against: backlog visibility, bonding capacity, margin discipline, and owner key-person risk—not SaaS multiples. Lead with **licensed operating history + regional demand**, then the growth plan and capital need.
